Common questions 🤔

How far will you travel?

Across the whole of Oxfordshire and into every surrounding county — Buckinghamshire, Berkshire, Gloucestershire, Warwickshire, Northamptonshire and Wiltshire. Longer journeys are usually fine for weddings and larger bookings; just ask.

How long do you stay at an event?

Whatever suits you. Two hours covers most parties, while weddings and fetes often run longer. Tell us your timings in the quote form and we'll price it up.

Can guests have free ice cream?

Yes. You can either pre-pay so everything is free to your guests, or we serve pay-as-you-go on the day. Plenty of weddings go for the pre-paid option.

Are you insured and DBS checked?

Yes. We hold £10m public liability cover, we are fully insured, we are DBS checked, and we hold a 5-star food hygiene rating. Venues and schools are welcome to request copies of anything before the booking.

What do you need at the venue?

Somewhere reasonably level to park with enough room to open the serving hatch. The van runs everything itself, so no power supply is needed.

Can you cover a really big event?

Yes. We can supply up to five vans for larger events, festivals and multi-site days, so queues stay short however many people are expected.

How far in advance should I book?

Summer weekends and wedding dates go early, so the sooner the better. Last-minute enquiries are still worth a call — we may have the date free.
